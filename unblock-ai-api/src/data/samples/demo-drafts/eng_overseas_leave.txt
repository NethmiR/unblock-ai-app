Any undergraduate student of the Faculty of Engineering who intends to travel
overseas during the academic term must obtain prior approval before leaving the
country.

The student submits a request stating their full name, registration number, the
destination country, the departure date, the return date, and the purpose of
travel.

The request goes first to the student's Academic Advisor. After the Academic
Advisor approves, it goes to the Head of the Department of Engineering, and
after that to the Faculty Coordinator for Student Affairs. Any approver may
approve, reject with a reason, or request more information from the student.

When all approvals are complete, the student receives a signed travel
authorization letter.
