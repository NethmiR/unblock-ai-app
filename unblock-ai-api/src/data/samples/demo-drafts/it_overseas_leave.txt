Any undergraduate student of the Faculty of Information Technology who intends
to travel overseas during the academic term must obtain prior approval before
leaving the country.

The student submits a request stating their full name, index number, the
destination country, the departure date, the return date, and the reason for
the trip.

The request first goes to the student's Academic Advisor for approval. Once the
Academic Advisor approves, it goes to the Head of the Department of Information
Technology. At any point an approver may either approve the request, reject it
with a reason, or ask the student for more information, in which case the
student is asked for the missing detail and the request returns to that same
approver.

If the total duration of the trip exceeds 30 days, the request must additionally
be endorsed by the Dean of the Faculty after the Head of Department has
approved.

When all required approvals are complete, the student is issued a signed
overseas leave authorization letter and notified that it is ready for
collection.
